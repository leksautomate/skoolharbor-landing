Refund Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

skoolHarbor gives you 3 free downloads before any payment. Please use the trial to confirm the extension works for the sites and courses you care about — that is what it is for. Completing a purchase means you accept the terms below.

skoolHarbor is a digital tool delivered instantly. Billing is processed by ExtensionPay (Stripe). This page sets out the narrow conditions under which a refund will be considered.

How long you have

A refund request must reach us within 14 days of the purchase date. Requests after that window are not reviewed. Monthly and Yearly plans can be cancelled at any time to stop future charges, but cancelling does not refund the billing period already in progress.

Every one of these must be true

  • The extension does not work at all, or its core advertised feature — detecting and downloading video as MP4 — is entirely broken for you.
  • The cause is skoolHarbor's own software, not a third-party site, your network, or your machine.
  • You are reporting it within the 14-day window.
  • You can show it — a screenshot or a short screen recording.
  • You raised the problem with us and gave a reasonable chance to fix it first.

Partial issues, one unsupported site, or a misunderstanding of what a feature does do not meet the bar.

Not eligible for a refund

  • Simply changing your mind, or buying the wrong plan by mistake.
  • Not having used the free trial to check compatibility first.
  • Skool, Vimeo, Loom, Wistia or YouTube changing their sites, going down, or blocking downloads.
  • A specific video being DRM-protected or otherwise not playable without protection in your browser.
  • Your account on a third-party platform being limited or restricted.
  • Using skoolHarbor in breach of its Terms of use.

Lifetime purchases

The Lifetime plan is a one-time purchase for ongoing access. The same 14-day window and conditions above apply. "Lifetime" means the supported lifetime of the product.

AppSumo codes

Licenses activated with an AppSumo code are governed by AppSumo's own refund policy, not this page. Request those refunds through AppSumo.

Requesting a refund

Open an issue on GitHub with your purchase email, the ExtensionPay/Stripe order ID, what went wrong, and proof of it. Missing information may lead to the request being declined.

Before you dispute a charge

Contact us first and allow a reasonable chance to resolve the issue before filing a bank dispute or chargeback. Filing a chargeback without doing so can result in your license being revoked and future purchases declined.

Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy overrides consumer-protection rights that apply to you by law.

Contact

Billing, cancellation and refund questions go to an issue on GitHub.
