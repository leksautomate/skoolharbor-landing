Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

skoolHarbor ("the extension") helps you find and download videos from Skool, Vimeo, Loom and Wistia pages you visit, and save them as MP4 files. This policy explains what the extension does and does not collect.

What the extension collects

Email address, for licensing only

To use the extension beyond the free trial, you register an email address through ExtensionPay, a third-party licensing service. Your email and your trial/paid status are sent to and stored by ExtensionPay so the extension can check whether you are entitled to unlimited downloads. skoolHarbor does not operate its own account system or server — ExtensionPay handles this on our behalf. See ExtensionPay's privacy policy for how they handle this data.

Payment itself (card details, billing) is handled entirely by ExtensionPay's hosted checkout page (backed by Stripe). The extension never sees or transmits your payment details.

AppSumo codes

If you redeem an AppSumo code, the code is sent to our licensing endpoint to validate it and mark that browser as licensed. No email or personal profile is created for code redemption.

What the extension does NOT do

  • It does not collect or transmit your browsing history, the videos you watch, or any page content to skoolHarbor's developer or any analytics service.
  • It does not use tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, or third-party ad networks.
  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone other than ExtensionPay, for the sole purpose described above.

How video detection and downloading works

All video detection (finding video URLs on pages you visit) and downloading (fetching video segments and combining them into an MP4) happens locally in your browser. Video files are fetched directly from the original hosting service — Skool's video CDN, Vimeo, Loom or Wistia — and saved to your computer using your browser's own downloads feature. None of this data passes through skoolHarbor's developer or any server we control.

Local storage

The extension stores a small amount of data locally in your browser (storage.local): the list of videos detected on your current tab, your download count, your install date, and your license state. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

Permissions

The extension requests host permissions matching Skool, Vimeo, Loom, Wistia, YouTube and their video CDNs so it can detect and download video content on those sites, and webRequest / declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess to identify video manifest URLs and set the request headers required by Skool's video CDN. These permissions are used only for the detection and download functionality described above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on GitHub.
