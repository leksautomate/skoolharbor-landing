How it works

Four steps, start to file

Install & open a lesson

Add skoolHarbor to Chrome or Firefox, then open a Skool lesson — or any Vimeo, Loom or Wistia page.

Play the video

Start playback so the player loads its stream. skoolHarbor detects it and adds it to the list.

Pick quality & select

Click the toolbar icon, choose a resolution where offered, and tick the videos you want.

Download Selected

Each video is stitched into a single MP4 in your browser and saved to your downloads folder.

Full walkthrough on the Guide page, including the yt-dlp tip for embedded YouTube.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Which sites does skoolHarbor support?

Skool lessons and posts (including Skool's own video CDN), plus Vimeo, Loom and Wistia pages. YouTube videos embedded in Skool lessons are surfaced with a ready-to-copy yt-dlp command rather than downloaded directly.

Do I need ffmpeg or any other software?

No. Detection and downloading happen entirely in your browser. HLS segments are fetched and combined into a single MP4 locally, then saved with your browser's own download manager.

Is it free?

Your first 3 downloads are free. After that you register an email to track your trial or subscribe for unlimited downloads — $7.99/mo, $69.99/yr, or $199 once for lifetime. AppSumo codes are redeemed directly in the popup.

Which browsers work?

Any Chromium browser with Manifest V3 support — Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc — through the Chrome Web Store, and Firefox 142 or newer through addons.mozilla.org.

Where do the downloads go?

Straight to your normal downloads folder via the browser's built-in download feature, named per video and saved as .mp4.

Does skoolHarbor send my data anywhere?

No browsing history, page content or video data leaves your machine. The only data transmitted is your email address, sent to ExtensionPay for licensing if you register beyond the free trial. See the Privacy Policy.

Can it grab DRM-protected video?

No. skoolHarbor only captures streams that already play in your browser without protection. It does not decrypt or circumvent DRM.

I have an AppSumo code — where does it go?

Open the popup, click Have an AppSumo code?, paste it in and redeem. The code activates on that browser directly; there is no separate login.
